Care Essentials

The Four Pillars of Healthy Plants

Master these fundamentals and your garden will thank you all year round.

Water Wisely

Check soil before watering. Deep, infrequent watering beats light daily sprinkles.

Right Light

Match each plant to the right light — bright and indirect is the sweet spot for most.

Quality Soil

Healthy roots need a mix that drains well and holds just the right amount of nutrients.

Prevent Pests

Inspect weekly, keep foliage dry, and quarantine new plants to stop problems early.
