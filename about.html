Our Story

From a Small Greenhouse to Your Garden

GreenNest started as a family-run greenhouse with a simple belief: everyone deserves to enjoy the calm and joy that plants bring. What began with a small collection of houseplants has grown into a trusted destination for plants, pots, soil, seeds, fertilizers, and tools.

Today, our team of horticulturists curates every product we sell, from hand-picked air-purifying plants to nutrient-rich organic soils. We stand behind everything with our Live Arrival Guarantee and a genuine passion for helping you grow.
